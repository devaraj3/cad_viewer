## [0.1.0] - 2026-04-15

### Added

- Initial project release of the browser-based CAD viewer.
- Route shell with landing page (`/`) and viewer experience (`/viewer`).
- CAD import support for STEP/STP, IGES/IGS, and BREP.
- Mesh and assembly support for STL, OBJ, 3MF, GLTF, and GLB.
- DXF preview support.
- Measurement tooling and section controls.
- Snapshot capture workflows (normal and outline).
